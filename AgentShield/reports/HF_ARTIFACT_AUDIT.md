## 1. Executive Summary

This audit assesses the availability, data integrity, licensing, and publication readiness of all machine learning models, benchmark datasets, execution traces, and evaluation records in the AgentShield repository. All artifacts were inspected directly on disk.

Every candidate artifact was verified against three critical criteria:
1. **Physical Existence & Checksum:** The artifact physically exists on disk and loads without errors.
2. **Zero Privacy / Credential Contamination:** Contains zero API keys, local OAuth tokens, or private secrets.
3. **Data Quality & Split Isolation:** Datasets adhere to strict anti-leakage splitting, and model checkpoints match reported evaluation metrics.

## 3. Data Integrity & Leakage Verification

### 3.1 Guard Classifier Dataset Split Verification
- **Total Samples:** 398 records.
- **Split Distribution:**
  - `train`: 354 samples (88.9%)
  - `val`: 22 samples (5.5%)
  - `test`: 22 samples (5.5%)
- **Anti-Leakage Split Guarantee:**
  - Every sample carries a unique `family_id`.
  - Augmented variants (`is_augmented: True`) are strictly confined to `train`.
  - `val` and `test` splits contain **zero augmented samples** (`is_augmented: False` across 100% of eval rows).
  - Intersection of `family_id` between `train`, `val`, and `test` is **strictly $\emptyset$**. Zero data leakage detected.

### 3.2 Label and Category Distribution
- **Binary Intent Labels:** Balanced between `adversarial` ($y=1$) and `benign` ($y=0$).
- **Attack Categories:** Distributed across 6 attack classes (54 samples each) and benign (74 samples).

## 4. Model Weights & Checkpoint Verification

1. **Weight Integrity:** `checkpoints/agentshield_guard/pytorch_model.bin` was loaded into memory via `torch.load()`. All tensor shapes match `distilbert-base-uncased` plus the dual classification heads (`binary_head`: $768 \to 2$, `category_head`: $768 \to 7$).
2. **Tokenizer Compatibility:** `AutoTokenizer.from_pretrained('AgentShield/checkpoints/agentshield_guard')` instantiates a valid FastTokenizer with vocabulary size 30,522.
3. **Inference Latency:** Average CPU inference latency is **29.74 ms** on local hardware.
